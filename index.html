Every set has a rival.

Rival puts what you lifted last time next to every set. Beat it and the ghost disappears. A three-tap check-in adjusts today's targets, and a body map shows which muscles got enough work this week.

Open the appSee how it works

On iPhone, open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It installs with its own icon and works offline.

Last week's you, on every set

The ghost is your last session of that exercise, set by set. Rival pre-fills a target just past it, so most sets take one tap: lift, tap the check, rest.

Matching the ghost counts too. It shows as matched, so a hard week still reads as holding the line rather than falling behind.

The rest timer starts on its own when you log a set, and the screen stays awake for the whole workout.

A bad night changes the plan, not the streak

Before you start, answer three questions about sleep, soreness and energy. Rival turns them into a score out of 100.

55 and above keeps the plan as written. Between 35 and 54, loads drop 5%. Below 35, loads drop 10% and the biggest lifts lose a set. Every change is shown in the workout, so you always know why a number moved.

See which muscles you're shortchanging

Every logged set counts toward the muscles it trains. A bench press set counts fully for chest and half for triceps and shoulders.

The body map shades each muscle against a target of 10 to 20 hard sets a week, the range commonly recommended for muscle growth. Gaps show up as pale patches, and too much volume turns red.

How Rival sets your next target

Each exercise has a rep range. Rival looks at your ghost and applies one of two rules, the same double progression most coaches use.

You hit the top of the range on every set

Add the smallest jump for that exercise, 2.5 kg on a barbell, and start back at the bottom of the range.

80 × 8, 8, 8→82.5 × 6, 6, 6

You didn't

Keep the weight and aim for one more rep on each set, up to the top of the range.

Everything else, one tap away

Made for the gym floor

Three tabs

Today, Progress and Plan. A workout opens full screen and tucks away with one tap.

Warm-ups built in

The first heavy lift for each muscle gets ramp-up sets, around 50%, 70% and 85% of your working weight, rounded to plates you own.

Big targets where it counts

The set inputs and the log button are 44 points tall, sized for chalky thumbs between sets.

Estimated 1-rep max

Each main lift gets a trend line, using weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30), so a heavy triple and a long set compare fairly.

kg or lb

Switch any time and your whole history converts.

Works offline

Install it to your home screen and it opens with no signal, in the basement gym or anywhere else. Back up your workouts to a file whenever you like.

Your ghost is already waiting.

Rival opens with a short guided tour and a sample week of training, so you can try every feature before logging your own. Clear it with one tap when you're ready.
